The Emerald Escapes philosophy

Some places are meant to be felt.

There are places that cannot be explained in words alone. They are felt in the breeze, discovered along ancient paths and kept in memories that last a lifetime. Ireland is one of those places.

Emerald Escapes is a fictional boutique travel concept created for curious travellers, couples and families who value authentic encounters, thoughtful itineraries and time to simply be.
